# Wincy clipboard

The third [concept study](concepts/03.png) supplied the silhouette for the final drawing. Its punched ring clears the top of the board, and the brown rim stays visible around the white sheet. Both details help the clipboard read in the Windows tray. The vector redraw warms the clip to brass, rounds the board corners and lifts a corner of the paper to reveal the previous sheet.

The four PNGs in `concepts/` were generated locally with Stable Diffusion Turbo from prompts for a soft dimensional illustration, a grainy flat print, an outlined sticker and an isometric study, in that order.

Use `mark.svg` for the full illustration and `mark-small.svg` at 16–32 px. The small drawing drops the dot grid, curled corner and narrow metal highlight. The light and dark lockups use outlined Schibsted Grotesk lettering. The editable desktop illustrations live in `backgrounds/`; the website serves their 2560 px WebP exports.
